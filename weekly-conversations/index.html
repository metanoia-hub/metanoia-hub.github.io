Συνουσία: The Metanoia Weekly Commons

A weekly commons where scientists, business leaders and master craftsmen think together from ground truth.

Part of the Metanoia Hub, Συνουσία is a small, recurring online gathering for people who see that the current societal and economic models are coming apart. We spend zero time analysing the decline itself. We are here to do the concrete work of figuring out what comes next, moving directly from discussion to physical application.

Συνουσία is the Greek word for being together. Plato wrote that the deepest understanding cannot be put into a book: it comes after long conversation among people who keep meeting, suddenly, like a spark that leaps across and lights a fire. That spark is what these meetings are for.

Who It's For

People whose knowledge comes from direct contact with reality: laboratories, fieldwork, workshops, production systems, and companies built under constraint.

  • Scientists from the whole range of research: biology, earth sciences, cosmology, physics, medicine, soil and water, agronomy. People with years of applied experience, inside or outside traditional academia, who have a coherent voice and practical evidence to share.
  • Business leaders from very successful backgrounds: CEOs, COOs and founders who understand that even successful companies cannot survive when the systemic conditions they were built on collapse, and who are ready to build across disciplines.
  • Master craftsmen in their own right: people who know how to make things and how to make them work, from stone and wood to metal, water and soil.

The Core Work: A 4-to-5-Month Runway

The weekly meetings are not an open-ended think tank. They are a rigorous preparation phase driving toward three integrated objectives:

  • Defining the Physical Ground: Boots on the ground means a large physical place we will work from, where real findings, experiments, consequences and learning can unfold. The weekly group helps make sure that place is chosen well: it stress-tests the operational plans, highlighting systemic requirements, environmental characteristics, and the blind spots that would otherwise turn into a big mistake.
  • Building Mτέλος (Mtelos): We are stripping away the modern clutter of disjointed facts to reorganise and recreate foundational knowledge. Mtelos is the working framework for rethinking human exchange, ecosystems, and daily life from the ground up: our own private language model, a library of what works on the ground, constantly updated by the people doing the work and reachable by anyone in their own language.
  • Structuring the Spring 2027 Greece Summit: The weekly discussions directly shape the agenda and problem sets for a month-long working convergence in Greece.

How A Meeting Works

The format is deliberately simple: a real case or observation, a disciplined discussion, and a synthesis of what we learned. We favour primary experience over abstraction, precise disagreement over polite agreement, and useful questions over finished answers. We ask that you come every week through the runway and bring something real.

The Greece Summit & Beyond

In Spring 2027, a larger ecosystem of CEOs, applied scientists, master craftsmen, and what I call new-economy architects (people who can rethink how we communicate and exchange) will gather in Greece. The participants of the weekly Συνουσία meetings are not serving as backstage prep staff for this event; they are the visionaries: they chart its course, step into the summit with a project already in motion, and are among the first on the ground.

Everything feeds into the physical execution. The weekly meetings forge the knowledge (Mtelos) and set the parameters for the Greece summit. The outcomes of that month-long summit provide the direct, stress-tested blueprint for the place. When we start work on the ground, the systemic web of life and the operational solutions will have already been concretely defined.
