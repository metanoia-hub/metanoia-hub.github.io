The Pioneers

From daydreaming to boots on the ground, from summer 2027.

Here we are moving from daydreaming to boots-on-the-ground implementation. Nobody has a perfect, proven way to do it. We will make mistakes, and we will also have a lot of joy and excitement working out how to even begin.

We all start from the same understanding. Society has broken up. People have woken up. We need a new way of life.

In practice, that means people with boots on the ground, organising a way of living that is completely free of the constraints and thinking we have today.

Where This Comes From

It comes straight from The Secret Manifesto: Creating Radically Innovative Ecosystems, which rests on three pillars:

  1. The Genius Fellowship: finding the people who are recovering lost knowledge.
  2. Transition Villages: people come together and start putting it into practice, looking for a way to live together with different economics, different governance, different goals and a different vision. Over time they grow into Radically Innovative Villages.
  3. Let's Rethink Life: the foundational knowledge that changes how we see life itself.

The Pioneers belong to Pillar Two. What follows is my first attempt at explaining what I can offer.

Is This You?

Maybe you've already tried to live differently. You joined a group or started one, and it fell apart, not because the idea was wrong but because it was not organised like a living system, so it could not live. You haven't given up. You're still looking for alternatives.

  • You are actively looking for a place to settle, not a nice way to spend your holidays.
  • You already get things done at an ordinary level at least: you have run a farm, a workshop, a business, a household or a team, and you have something you want to bring to the group.
  • You are strong in one of the essentials: water, soil, food, energy, building, health, organisation, infrastructure set-up, exchange, governance.
  • You bring something: skills, tools, a trade, people, or means.
  • You can lead in your own field and still answer to the core where the buck stops.
  • You have things you want to learn.

This is not for you if you are looking to escape, to be looked after, or for a retreat; if you want everything put to a vote, or a guru; if your goals are unclear, if you are after power, or if your emotions run you.

Two Kinds Of Pioneers

  • Those who settle: they lead their field and teach. The first group is made of these.
  • Those who come to learn: some are needed, but not all of them.

The pioneers have their own meetings and their own space, separate from the Συνουσία meetings of scientists, craftsmen and business leaders, because the level and the interest are different.

What You Get

  • A place on the land from summer 2027, among the first group working one piece of ground together.
  • A place organised like a living biological system: a small core where the buck stops, with rings around it gathering information, so it does not fall apart like the places you may have tried before.
  • The foundational knowledge, so we do not fall back into the same old ways of running things.
  • A role you choose yourself, with access to the experts of Συνουσία.

How It Goes

  1. The launch. We open with an online evening: the films are shown, then everyone splits into small groups to talk. You are invited to comment on them, expand them or clarify them.
  2. The pioneer meetings. Online, roughly every two weeks, just for the pioneers. The question at the heart of them: are you looking to move to a place built as a different way of life? If so, how, and why? This is where you understand what we are building, share your experience with others like you, and help set the scene for the summer. If it turns out this is not what you want, that is fine: it saves time, money and energy on both sides.
  3. Getting in touch. Book a call, ask your questions, tell us who you are and what you are actually looking for.
  4. Greece, spring 2027. A month-long gathering. A session with the pioneers, before, after or woven into it, is being worked out.
  5. On the land, summer 2027. 100 to 120 people for 2–3 months, in a large physical place we will work from.
  6. Winter. The group shrinks to 25–30: those who are really capable and want to carry on.

Only want to have a look? There will be a few days in Greece, or on the land itself, for visitors.

The Terms

  • It is a paying commitment.
  • You are not paid: this is not a job and not a workforce.
  • It is not free: this is not WWOOF or Workaway, where people work in exchange for their keep. We are not here to exploit your work.
  • You come to check whether the way I propose this makes sense to you. Feedback is welcome; debating the grand idea is not what this is for.
  • Coming to the online pioneer meetings beforehand is a condition. Nobody should turn up in summer and feel lost or cheated because they did not do their homework.
  • Summer stays are 2–3 months at most.
  • Accommodation will be basic at best. That is why you are pioneers.
  • Health, insurance and the like: to be set out.
  • Families with young children: not this summer. There are no safeguards and no organisation for it yet. Individual situations can be looked at.

Is It Real Or Just A Summer?

  • Winter is the test: you commit to more than the summer.
  • You are already moving: selling, leaving a job, looking for land.
  • You can say what you bring. A visitor brings a backpack.
  • You can say clearly why your last place failed.
  • You keep turning up to the pioneer meetings.

The Films

Drawn from the Manifesto, they are the starting point for the meetings.

1. Why Most Places Fail, And What The Outcome Is

There is no mystery why so many ecovillages fail: they run on luck, with no clear purpose. The village is not built only to survive, but to make life fulfilling and enjoyable for everyone in it. Everyone who comes in agrees on a clear outcome, with room to adjust when events push it off course.

2. How The Village Is Led

A genuine leader who sees the big picture and the steps to get there; no leaderless structure, no endless sociocracy meetings. Ideas come up from everyone; if there is no quick agreement, the leader decides. An inner core of three, with rings around it (food, education, production, energy) who step in if the leader falls. It copies the patterns of the living world: cells, rings, mycelium.

3. Who Gets In, And How

Selection is fierce: one bad apple spoils the group. The skills that cannot be missing come first: ten surgeons and nobody who can dig a swale does not work. A trial period comes before becoming part of the village, with a clear and calm way to leave.

Tell Us About Yourself

Three questions to start with. How you answer tells us how much you have thought about it:

  • Is there anything you want to ask us?
  • Is there anything you should tell us?
  • What have you lived through that is important for us to know, so we can have a conversation?

And, if you want to go further:

  • When you think of joining a group of people creating a new way to live together, what are you looking for?
  • If you were somewhere that fell apart, what caused it?
  • What is not negotiable for you?
  • What have you got to bring to the table?
  • What do you want to learn?
  • Why now? What is pushing you to move?
  • How long do you see yourself staying: one summer, a few years, the rest of your life?
  • What have you already done with your hands: grown food, built, repaired, kept animals?
  • What do you do when you disagree with a decision you did not make?
  • How do you handle hard physical work, bad weather, and living without your usual comforts?
  • Who would come with you (partner, children, animals)?
  • What would make you leave?
  • What do you think a leader is for?
  • What can you put in, apart from your work: money, tools, equipment, contacts?
  • What have you done to understand how living systems work (reading, courses, your own practice)?
