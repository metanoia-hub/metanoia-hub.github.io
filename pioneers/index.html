The Pioneers

From daydreaming to boots on the ground, from summer 2027.

Here we are moving from daydreaming to boots-on-the-ground implementation. Nobody has a perfect, proven way to do it. We will make mistakes, and we will also have a lot of joy and excitement working out how to even begin.

We all start from the same understanding. Society has broken up. People have woken up. We need a new way of life.

In practice, that means people with boots on the ground, organising a way of living that is completely free of the constraints and thinking we have today.

Where This Comes From

It comes straight from The Secret Manifesto: Creating Radically Innovative Ecosystems, which rests on three pillars:

  1. The Genius Fellowship: finding the people who are recovering lost knowledge.
  2. Transition Villages: people come together and start putting it into practice, looking for a way to live together with different economics, different governance, different goals and a different vision. Over time they grow into Radically Innovative Villages.
  3. Let's Rethink Life: the foundational knowledge that changes how we see life itself.

The Pioneers belong to Pillar Two. What follows is my first attempt at explaining what I can offer.

Is This You?

Maybe you've already tried to live differently. You joined a group or started one, and it fell apart, not because the idea was wrong but because nobody had worked out how such a place actually runs. You haven't given up. You're still looking.

  • You want to settle for the long term, not spend a nice summer away.
  • You've done real things: grown food, built, run a business, a farm or a team.
  • You have something to bring, and things you want to learn.

What It Is, Plainly

  • It starts in summer 2027, in a large physical place we will work from.
  • The first summer is 2–3 months. Living conditions will be basic. That's why we call you pioneers.
  • It's a paying commitment. You aren't hired, and it isn't a free working holiday.
  • It isn't yet the right time for families with young children.

How It Starts

We meet online about every two weeks. That's where you find out what we're building and whether it's for you, and where you help shape the first summer. Nobody comes in summer without having been part of these meetings.
